FAQ

Questions we hear most

Which industries do you work with?

Solar farms, onshore and offshore wind farms, and mining operations. We support each across the full asset lifecycle: pre-construction surveys, construction monitoring, and ongoing operations and maintenance.

How is a drone inspection better than a manual one?

It is faster, safer and more precise. A drone can inspect 20+ turbines a day against 1–2 manually, survey 500+ hectares in hours rather than weeks, and it does so without anyone working at height or entering an active zone. AI analysis then catches defects that visual checks routinely miss.

What do I receive at the end?

Clear, actionable deliverables: thermal anomaly maps, blade condition reports, stockpile volume inventories, orthomosaics, 3D terrain models and a prioritised maintenance plan — every finding GPS-tagged and ranked by urgency.

Where do you operate?

We are headquartered in Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, in India's renewable energy corridor, and take on projects across India.
